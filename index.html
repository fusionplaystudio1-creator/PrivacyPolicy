Privacy Policy

Fusion Play Studio builds mobile games. This page explains, plainly, what information our games process and why — so you know exactly where you stand before you play.

Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number or home address. We use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand gameplay, improve stability and enhance the player experience. Through Firebase Analytics, Google may process the following technical information on our behalf:

  • Device or other identifiers — such as the Firebase Installation ID or other identifiers used by Firebase Analytics to measure app usage
  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address (country/region level only, not precise GPS location)
  • App activity — such as screens viewed, session duration, level starts/completions and other in-app interactions
  • Device information — device model, operating system version and language settings

How We Use It

  • Improve gameplay and balancing
  • Fix crashes and technical issues
  • Monitor performance and stability
  • Understand anonymous player engagement

Third‑Party Services

This application uses Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how players use our game. Information processed through Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy documentation. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any third party beyond Google's own processing of it as our analytics provider.

Google Privacy Policy  ·  Firebase Privacy

Data Security & Retention

Analytics data is processed and retained according to the data retention settings configured in our Firebase project and Google's applicable policies. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, although no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may be able to limit certain analytics or advertising-related data collection through your device or Google account privacy settings. Available options may vary depending on your device and operating system.

Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will investigate promptly.
