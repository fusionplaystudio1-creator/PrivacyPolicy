Privacy Policy

Fusion Play Studio builds mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use our games, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Information We May Process

We do not intentionally ask you to provide personally identifying information such as your name, home address, or phone number through normal gameplay. Our games use analytics and advertising services that may automatically process certain technical and usage information, including:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as app/device identifiers used for analytics, advertising, fraud prevention, and measurement.
  • Approximate location, which may be inferred from your IP address. We do not request precise GPS location for these purposes.
  • App activity, such as sessions, screen views, level starts/completions, gameplay interactions, and ad interactions.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and similar technical information.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash, performance, and error information where supported by the services used in the game.

How We Use Information

  • Understand how players use the game and improve gameplay.
  • Measure progression, engagement, and game performance.
  • Detect and troubleshoot crashes, errors, and technical problems.
  • Serve, measure, and improve advertising where advertising is enabled.
  • Help prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — used to understand gameplay and app usage.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — used to display and measure advertisements.
  • Unity Ads — may be used as an advertising source through AdMob mediation.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and the configuration of their SDKs in the app.

Google Privacy Policy  ·  Firebase Privacy and Security  ·  Unity Privacy Policy

Advertising and Consent

Where required by applicable law or platform rules, our game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy or consent choices before advertising-related processing. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or subject to other limited processing.

In regions where a privacy-options entry point is required, the game may provide a Privacy Choices or similar option in its settings so you can review or update your choices.

Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising and analytics providers may, however, process or share certain information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and related purposes. Some privacy laws may define certain advertising-related disclosures as a “sale” or “sharing.” Where applicable, you can use the privacy choices presented in the app to opt out or change your preferences.

Data Retention and Security

Information handled by third-party services is retained according to the retention settings and policies of those providers and the settings configured for our app. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

Depending on your region, device, and applicable law, you may be able to limit certain analytics or advertising-related processing through the privacy choices shown in the game, your device settings, or your Google account settings.

Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our game, SDKs, advertising setup, analytics setup, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
